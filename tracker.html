Fixed monthly costs

These run every month regardless of sales — rent, utilities, wifi, etc. Set the monthly amount once and the tracker automatically spreads it across each day (monthly ÷ days in that month) and folds it into every day's expenses and profit, so you don't have to re-enter it. Add labour yourself each day on the Daily Entry tab since it changes daily.

Delivery commission rate

Uber Eats, DoorDash, and Skip take a cut of every order. This applies one flat rate to all revenue collected through those three channels (from the Payment Channels numbers) — it doesn't currently distinguish delivery vs. pickup orders within an app, so it's a conservative estimate on days with pickup-through-app orders.
