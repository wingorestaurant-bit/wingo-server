Flavour FAQ

How many wing flavours does Wing-O have?
Wing-O's menu lists 131 wing sauces and dry rubs in 8 groups, from mild classics like Honey Garlic to Dead Angel (Carolina Reaper).
What is the hottest wing at Wing-O?
Dead Angel, made with Carolina Reaper, is our hottest sauce. Spicy Mammilla (Caesar + Dead Angel + Ranch) and Devil's Kiss are also very hot.
What are the most popular Wing-O flavours?
Honey Garlic, Honey Dill, Buffalo, Salt & Pepper and Lemon Pepper are guest favourites. Ask for the flavour of the week in store.
Does Wing-O have dry rub wings?
Yes. The Rub It group has 25+ dry rubs, including Salt & Vinegar, Dill Pickle, Lemon Pepper, Cajun and Montreal Style.
Can I get two flavours in one order?
Each order gets one flavour. With two or more orders you can pick a different flavour for each, and our combo sauces (Second Round and Illegal Sauces) mix two or three sauces for you.
