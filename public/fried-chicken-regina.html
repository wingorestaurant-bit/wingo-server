Where to Get Fried Chicken in Regina

📅 Updated September 2026 · ⏱️ 4 min read

When a fried chicken craving hits in Regina, you want it crispy, hot and fast. At Wing-O, everything chicken is fried to order and finished with your choice of 135+ sauces and dry rubs. Here's how to get your fix.

Crispy bone-in chicken wings

Our signature. Fried until the skin crackles, then tossed in anything from Honey Garlic to Devil's Kiss (Carolina Reaper). A half order is $7.99 and a full order is $13.99.

Boneless wings

All the crunch, none of the bones — and every flavour works. Half $7.99, full $13.99.

Chicken fingers

Crispy, juicy chicken fingers with your choice of dipping sauce. Half $7.99, full $13.99 — a favourite with kids and anyone who likes to dunk.

Fried chicken burgers

Hand-breaded chicken breast on a toasted bun, served with fries and a drink:

  • Korean Fried Chicken (KFC) — scratch-made Korean BBQ sauce, coleslaw and pickles. $16.49
  • The Tough Guy — double-crunched and coated in blazing Carolina Reaper sauce. $18.99
  • The Dillinator — honey dill sauce and pickle chips. $18.99
  • The Buffalo Stampede — house-made buffalo sauce and creamy coleslaw. $18.99
  • The Sunset Crunch — sweet heat habanero mango and California ranch. $15.99

Late-night fried chicken in Regina

Our Regina locations stay open until 1 AM Monday to Wednesday and 3 AM Thursday to Sunday — so fried chicken after the bar or a late shift is covered. Order online for pickup, or get delivery anywhere in Regina for a flat $5.50.
