💌 Win-back emails

Once a day at 11 AM, customers whose last online order was a while ago get one email with a discount. It comes off by itself when they order online with that email or phone number. Nobody gets it more than once in the repeat period, and everyone who unsubscribed from any Wing-O email is left out.

⏱️ Automatic wait time

The “ready in” time customers see online follows how busy the kitchen is: Clover orders from the last 20 minutes, all channels. The wait picked in /admin becomes the minimum. Updates every 2 minutes.

🔄 Clover sold-out sync

When staff mark an item “not available” in Clover, the matching website items are sold out online and crossed out on the TVs. When it's available again in Clover they come back. Checks every 2 minutes. First match each website item to its Clover item (suggestions are filled in for you).
