OFFICIAL CONTEST RULES

"Free Wings For A Year" Promotional Contest · Wing-O Restaurants Ltd.

READ CAREFULLY. By entering this contest you agree to be bound by these Official Rules and the decisions of the Sponsor, which are final and binding in all matters relating to the contest.

1. Sponsor

This contest (the "Contest") is sponsored by Wing-O Restaurants Ltd. (the "Sponsor"), located at #3 – 155 Albert St N, Regina, Saskatchewan. Email: besaucy@wingorestaurants.com.

2. No Purchase Necessary

NO PURCHASE OR PAYMENT OF ANY KIND IS REQUIRED TO ENTER OR WIN. A purchase or payment will not increase a participant's chances of winning.

3. Eligibility

The Contest is open only to legal residents of Saskatchewan, Canada who, at the time of entry, have reached the age of majority in their province of residence (18 years of age or older). The following are not eligible to enter or win: employees, officers, directors, agents, and representatives of the Sponsor, their immediate family members (spouse, parents, siblings, children), and persons living in the same household as such individuals, whether or not related.

Void where prohibited by law. The Contest is subject to all applicable federal, provincial, and municipal laws and regulations.

4. Contest Period

The Contest begins at 12:00:01 AM Central Standard Time (CST) on [OPEN DATE] and ends at 11:59:59 PM CST on [CLOSE DATE] (the "Contest Period"). The Sponsor's computer is the official time-keeping device for the Contest.

5. How to Enter

One (1) base entry per person. To receive your base entry, complete the entry form at wingorestaurants.com/contest with all required information (name, email, phone, city, province) and submit it during the Contest Period.

Bonus entries (optional): Each of the following actions, when completed during the Contest Period and verified by the Sponsor, will earn one (1) additional entry, to a maximum of three (3) bonus entries per person (4 total entries):

  • Following @wingorestaurants on Instagram (provide your IG handle).
  • Following Wing-O on Facebook (provide your FB name).
  • Tagging at least one friend on the Sponsor's official Instagram contest post (provide the tagged handle).

The Sponsor reserves the right to verify bonus entries and disqualify entries that cannot be verified.

Limit one (1) entrant per email address and per phone number. Multiple submissions from the same person will be consolidated into a single entry record.

6. Prize

One (1) Grand Prize is available, consisting of: one (1) free wing order per calendar week for fifty-two (52) consecutive weeks, redeemable in-person at any of the Sponsor's five (5) corporate-owned locations (Albert Street, Rochdale, East Regina, Moose Jaw, Regina Beach). Redemption is not available at franchise locations.

Prize details:

  • "Wing order" means one (1) order of bone-in wings, boneless wings, or chicken fingers from the standard menu.
  • One redemption per calendar week (Monday 12:00 AM CST through Sunday 11:59 PM CST). Unused weekly redemptions do not carry forward.
  • Prize period begins the calendar week following successful claim of the prize.
  • Winner must present the phone number registered with the entry at the time of each redemption.
  • Prize is non-transferable. Cash equivalent is not available.

Approximate Retail Value (ARV): The ARV of the Grand Prize is approximately CAD $725.00 (based on 52 weekly orders at the standard menu price of one order of wings). Actual value may vary based on the winner's redemption pattern.

7. Skill-Testing Question

In accordance with Section 74.06 of the Competition Act (Canada), the selected winner must, without assistance of any kind, correctly answer a time-limited, mathematical skill-testing question administered by the Sponsor before being declared the official Grand Prize winner. The question will take the form of a multi-step arithmetic problem (for example: "(25 × 4) + 12 − 7 = ?") and will be presented at the time of winner notification. The winner will have three (3) attempts and seventy-two (72) hours from notification to submit a correct answer. Failure to do so will result in disqualification and the random selection of an alternate potential winner.

8. Selection of Winner

One (1) potential Grand Prize winner will be selected by random draw from all eligible entries received during the Contest Period. The draw will be conducted on [DRAW DATE] at the Sponsor's headquarters in Regina, Saskatchewan. The number of eligible entries determines the odds of winning.

9. Winner Notification and Claim

The potential winner will be notified by telephone and email using the contact information provided at entry. The potential winner must:

  1. Respond to the notification within seven (7) days of the first notification attempt;
  2. Correctly answer the skill-testing question;
  3. Sign a Declaration and Release form confirming eligibility and agreeing to the use of their name, city of residence, and likeness for promotional purposes without further compensation.

If the potential winner cannot be reached, fails to respond, fails the skill-testing question, or is otherwise found to be ineligible, the prize will be forfeited and an alternate potential winner will be selected by random draw from the remaining eligible entries.

10. Privacy

Personal information collected from entrants will be used solely for the administration of this Contest (including verification of eligibility, notification of the winner, and announcement of the winner) unless the entrant has expressly consented to additional uses (such as receiving commercial electronic messages). The Sponsor complies with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL). Entrants may request access to or deletion of their personal information by contacting besaucy@wingorestaurants.com.

  • To these Official Rules and the decisions of the Sponsor, which are final and binding;
  • To release and hold harmless the Sponsor and its officers, directors, employees, and agents from any and all liability, loss, or damage arising from participation in the Contest or acceptance, use, or misuse of the prize;
  • That the Sponsor is not responsible for entries that are late, lost, incomplete, misdirected, illegible, undelivered, or affected by technical malfunction;
  • That the Sponsor reserves the right to disqualify any entrant who tampers with the entry process, violates these Rules, or acts in a disruptive or unsportsmanlike manner;
  • That the Sponsor reserves the right to cancel, suspend, or modify the Contest if any factor impairs its integrity or proper administration, including fraud, virus, or other technical failure.

12. Winner Announcement

The name and city of the confirmed Grand Prize winner will be posted on wingorestaurants.com and the Sponsor's Instagram and Facebook pages within fourteen (14) days of prize confirmation. To request the winner's name by mail, send a self-addressed stamped envelope to: Wing-O Restaurants Ltd., Attn: Free Wings For A Year Contest, #3 – 155 Albert St N, Regina, SK, S4R 3N5.

13. Governing Law

This Contest is governed by the laws of the Province of Saskatchewan and the laws of Canada applicable therein, without regard to conflict of laws principles. Any dispute arising out of or relating to this Contest shall be resolved exclusively in the courts of Saskatchewan.
