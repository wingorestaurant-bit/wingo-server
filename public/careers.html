Common Questions

FAQ

Do I need previous experience?
No experience needed for kitchen or cashier roles — we train. For supervisor/manager positions, prior restaurant management experience is required.
What's the minimum age to work at WingO?
16 for front-of-house and kitchen. Delivery drivers must be 18+ with a valid license.
Do I need to know how to make wings before applying?
Nope. We teach the Sauce Boss way from scratch. What we look for: attitude, work ethic, teamwork.
How long does the hiring process take?
Usually 1-2 weeks from application to first shift. We aim to respond to every application within 5 business days.
Are you hiring for Winnipeg?
Yes — Winnipeg is opening soon and we need the full crew. Kitchen, cashier, delivery, supervisors, and a General Manager. Ground-floor opportunity.
What are the hours like?
WingO is open 11 AM – 1 AM (Mon-Wed) and 11 AM – 3 AM (Thu-Sun). Shifts are typically 6-8 hours. Late-night shifts are available (great for tips).
Do you offer any benefits beyond wages?
Free staff meals every shift, tips shared across the team, flexible scheduling, and real career growth opportunities as we expand to Winnipeg and beyond.
