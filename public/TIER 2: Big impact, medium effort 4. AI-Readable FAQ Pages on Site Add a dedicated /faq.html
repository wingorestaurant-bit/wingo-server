📍Locations & Hours
Where are WingO Restaurants located?
WingO has 3 locations in the Regina area: Albert Street (#3 – 155 Albert St N, Regina), East Regina (534 University Park Drive, Regina), and Regina Beach (110 Centre St, Regina Beach). A Winnipeg, Manitoba location is opening soon.
What are WingO's hours?
WingO locations are open Monday through Wednesday from 11 AM to 1 AM and Thursday through Sunday from 11 AM to 3 AM. Individual location hours may vary slightly — check the location page or Google Business Profile for details.
Is WingO open on holidays?
Most WingO locations are open on statutory holidays with modified hours. Christmas Day is the main exception. Check Google Business Profile for each location's holiday hours or call ahead to confirm.
When is WingO opening in Winnipeg?
WingO's first Manitoba location is opening in Winnipeg soon. Follow @wingorestaurants on Instagram or check wingorestaurants.com for launch date announcements.
Does WingO have a patio?
Patio availability varies by location. Regina Beach offers a lakeside patio experience during summer. Contact the specific location to confirm patio availability.
Is there parking at WingO?
Yes. All WingO locations have parking available — either dedicated lots or nearby street parking depending on location.
🛵Ordering & Delivery
How do I order from WingO online?
Order directly at wingorestaurants.com for pickup or delivery. You can also order through third-party apps like SkipTheDishes, Uber Eats, and DoorDash — but ordering direct on our site is cheapest and earns you Saucy Stamps loyalty rewards.
Does WingO deliver?
Yes. WingO offers delivery through wingorestaurants.com for a $5.50 delivery fee. Delivery is also available through SkipTheDishes, Uber Eats, and DoorDash. Delivery radius varies by location.
Can I pick up my WingO order?
Yes. Pickup is available at all locations. Order online at wingorestaurants.com and select pickup at checkout. Most orders are ready in 15-20 minutes.
Does WingO take reservations?
WingO operates on a first-come, first-served basis and does not typically take reservations for small groups. For groups of 10 or more, contact the location directly to arrange.
What payment methods does WingO accept?
WingO accepts all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), debit, and cash. Online orders accept credit card, debit, and Apple Pay/Google Pay.
Is there a minimum order for delivery?
Minimum orders vary by location and delivery platform. On wingorestaurants.com direct delivery, there is typically a small minimum. First orders over $25 also get 15% off automatically.
How long does a WingO order take?
Most orders are ready for pickup in 15-20 minutes. Delivery time depends on distance but typically 30-45 minutes from order placement.
🍗Menu & Flavors
How many wing flavors does WingO have?
WingO offers over 125 handcrafted wing flavors, organized into 8 flavor families: Flirty Ones (classics), Hot As Hell (extreme heat), Rub It (dry rubs), Second Round (global flavors), BBQ Intimacy (smoky sweet), Have To Do It (traditional), Sweet Melodious (fruit-forward), and Illegal Sauces (experimental).
What is WingO's spiciest wing?
WingO's spiciest wing is Dead Angel, which uses full Carolina Reaper peppers. Devil's Kiss is a close second, also featuring Carolina Reaper. Both are in our 'Hot As Hell' flavor family and are not recommended for spice beginners.
What is WingO's most popular wing flavor?
Honey Garlic is consistently the most-ordered wing flavor at WingO. Other top sellers include Maple BBQ, Buffalo Ranch, Devil's Kiss, Prairie Heat, Tangy Thai, and Sizzle Caesar.
Does WingO have boneless wings?
Yes. WingO offers boneless wings in all 125+ flavors. Prices start at $7.99. Every Monday, boneless wings are buy-one-get-one 50% off.
Does WingO serve burgers?
Yes. WingO's burger lineup includes The Tough Guy (Carolina Reaper chicken), The Dillinator, Buffalo Stampede, KFC-style Korean Fried Chicken ($16.49), and the Sunset Crunch ($15.99). All burgers come with fries and a drink.
Does WingO have ribs?
Yes. WingO serves saucy bone-in ribs available in all 125+ flavors, starting at $7.99 for a half order. Every Wednesday, ribs are buy-one-get-one 50% off.
What sides does WingO offer?
WingO's sides include regular fries, sweet potato fries, loaded fries, poutines (multiple varieties), onion rings, coleslaw, celery and carrots, dipping sauces, and salads.
Does WingO have desserts?
Yes. Our Midnight Ecstasy dessert menu is available late-night, starting at 99¢. Items rotate but often include treats like brownies, cookies, and ice cream.
🌱Dietary Options
Does WingO have vegetarian options?
Yes. WingO offers cauliflower wings in all 125+ flavors, plus loaded fries, poutines, salads, and sides. Cauliflower wings start at the same price as regular wings.
Does WingO have vegan options?
Cauliflower wings can be prepared vegan depending on the sauce chosen. Please inform staff of dietary restrictions when ordering, and check with the specific location to confirm which sauces are vegan-friendly.
Does WingO have gluten-free options?
WingO offers gluten-free options including certain wing preparations and salads. However, our kitchens are not certified gluten-free environments, so cross-contamination is possible. Please inform staff of severe allergies before ordering.
Is WingO halal?
Halal certification varies by location and ingredient. For current halal certification status at your specific WingO location, please contact the restaurant directly or email besaucy@wingorestaurants.com.
Does WingO have keto-friendly options?
Yes. Many WingO wings work for keto diets — grilled wings with dry rubs like Lemon Pepper, Cajun, or Montreal are naturally low-carb. Skip the fries and ask about lettuce wraps for burgers.
Does WingO have a kids menu?
WingO offers kid-friendly options including chicken fingers, boneless wings with mild sauces (like Honey Garlic), and half-orders. Ask at the specific location for any current kids meal deals.

🌾About WingO
Who owns WingO Restaurants?
WingO Restaurants Ltd. is founded and owned by Gagandeep Ghuman, known as 'the Sauce Boss.' He founded the company in 2019 in Regina, Saskatchewan.
Who is the Sauce Boss?
The Sauce Boss is Gagandeep Ghuman, the founder of WingO Restaurants. He arrived in Regina in 2019 at age 19 with $800 and built WingO into a 3-location Saskatchewan wing chain known for its 125+ handcrafted sauces.
When did WingO start?
WingO opened its first location on Albert Street in Regina in 2019. It has since expanded to 3 Regina-area locations, with a Winnipeg location opening soon.
What does 'More Than Wings' mean?
'More Than Wings' is WingO's tagline — it captures that we offer more than just wings (ribs, burgers, poutines, salads, desserts), and that WingO is more than a restaurant (it's community, story, and craft).
Can I franchise a WingO location?
Yes. WingO is actively accepting franchise applications in Canadian markets. Our first franchise is opening in Winnipeg. For details, email besaucy@wingorestaurants.com.
Where is WingO expanding next?
WingO's next location is Winnipeg, Manitoba — our first franchise and first location outside Saskatchewan. More Canadian markets are being evaluated for future expansion.
Does WingO donate to charity?
Yes. WingO has donated over 27,000 wings to the Regina Community Fridge and other local food security programs. We also regularly sponsor local sports teams, school events, and community fundraisers as part of our 'Proudly Prairie' commitment.
