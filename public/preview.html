THE SAUCE BOSS

Hey you. Yeah, you with the sauce on your thumb.

We started Wing-O because wings deserve better than “mild, medium, hot.” Now we're in Regina, Regina Beach and Winnipeg, and the rule hasn't changed: toss it fresh, sauce it loud, and never send out a wing we wouldn't eat at 2 AM ourselves.

Here are my picks this week. Don't tell the other sauces.
