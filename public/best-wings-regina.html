Where to Get the Best Chicken Wings in Regina (2026 Guide)

📅 Updated November 2026 · ⏱️ 6 min read

Regina takes wings seriously. Honey Dill is a prairie institution, and everyone has an opinion on crispy versus saucy. This guide covers what makes a great wing, how to order at Wing-O, every one of our 131 flavours sorted by heat, and the best deal for every day of the week.

What makes a great wing

Crisp first. Every wing at Wing-O is fried to order in halal-certified beef tallow, so the skin crackles before it ever meets sauce. Sauce second. Wings are tossed in the bowl right before they go out, so the sauce coats instead of soaking. Choice third. With 135+ flavours, from sweet glazes to dry rubs to Carolina Reaper, there's no such thing as "the usual" unless you want one.

FAQ

What are the best wings in Regina?
Wing-O is a Regina favourite for wings: 135+ flavours, crispy bone-in and boneless wings from $7.99, a deal every day of the week and two Regina locations open until 3 AM Thursday to Sunday.
How many wings are in an order at Wing-O?
A full order is 9 wings and a half order is smaller. You can choose a different flavour for each order when you get two or more.
How much are wings at Wing-O Regina?
A half order is $7.99 and a full order is $13.99, for bone-in or boneless. Sunday wings are 89¢ each (minimum 10), and Friday is 3 orders for $30.
What is the hottest wing at Wing-O?
Dead Angel, made with Carolina Reaper. Spicy Mammilla (Caesar, Dead Angel and Ranch) and Devil's Kiss are close behind.
Does Wing-O have dry rubs?
Yes. The Rub It list has dry seasonings like Salt & Pepper, Lemon Pepper, Dill Pickle and Cajun, shaken on with no sauce. All 131 sauces and rubs are on the flavours page.
Does Wing-O have Honey Dill wings?
Yes. Honey Dill is on the menu, along with Creamy Dill Pickle and the Dill Pickle dry rub.
Where are Wing-O locations in Regina?
Wing-O is at #3 – 155 Albert St N and 534 University Park Drive in Regina, plus a summer location at 110 Centre St in Regina Beach.
What are Wing-O wings fried in?
Wings, boneless wings, ribs, chicken fingers and chicken burgers are fried in halal-certified beef tallow. Fries, cauliflower wings and other sides use a separate canola-oil fryer.
Is Wing-O open late?
Yes. Regina locations are open until 1 AM Monday to Wednesday and 3 AM Thursday to Sunday. Online orders after 11 PM get 10% off.
Does Wing-O deliver in Regina?
Yes. Order at wingorestaurants.com for delivery anywhere in Regina for a flat $5.50, or pickup in about 20 minutes.
Is there a wing night in Regina?
Thursday is wing night at Wing-O: buy an order of wings and get the second 50% off. Tuesday is all-you-can-eat wings for $28.99 (dine-in, 5–10 PM).
